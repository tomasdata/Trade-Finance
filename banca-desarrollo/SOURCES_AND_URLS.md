### 🇨🇱 **CHILE**

#### CORFO (Corporación de Fomento de la Producción)
- **Role:** Manages guarantee programs (COBEX, FOGAIN), enables ~30-40% of SME exports
- **Website:** https://www.corfo.cl
- **Key Programs:**
  - COBEX: Guarantees for small/medium exporters
  - FOGAIN: Agricultural guarantee fund
  - Pro-Inversión: Investment promotion
- **Monthly Guarantee Reports:** https://www.corfo.cl/web/guest/descargas
  - Pattern: "Informe de Garantías [Mes] [Año]"
  - Available: Most months 2023-2024
- **Data to Extract:**
  - Guarantee volumes by bank
  - Guarantee coverage by firm size (SME distribution)
  - Regional distribution (key for inequal access analysis)
  - Implicit guarantee multiplier (how much private TF was enabled)
- **Expected File Size:** 200-500 KB per monthly report
- **Download Status:** ⏳ Monthly reports available (search website)

## 🛠️ How to Download Manually

### For Most Institutions:
1. Go to institution website
2. Navigate to "Publicaciones," "Informes," "Reportes," or "Descargas"
3. Search for "2023" annual report or equivalent
4. Save PDF to corresponding folder in `banca-desarrollo/[país]/[institución]/`

### For Blocked Sources:
- **COFIDE (Perú):** Contact directly (contacto@cofide.com.pe) - firewall blocks automated access
- **FNG (Colombia):** Incapsula protection - try access from different IP or request via email
- **CABEI (Central America):** React-based website - requires manual browser navigation

### For Monthly Reports:
- **CORFO (Chile):** Pattern "Informe de Garantías [Mes] [Año]" - available 2023-2024
- Save all available months to `chile/corfo/` folder

## 📈 Data Extraction Priorities

Once PDFs are downloaded, extract in this order:

### **Priority 1: Trade Finance Core Metrics**
1. **Portfolio Size** (by year, in USD equivalent)
   - Total TF outstanding
   - New disbursements
   - By product: LC, Export credit, guarantees, factoring

2. **Firm Size Distribution**
   - % of TF to SMEs vs. large firms
   - Comparison with private bank concentration

3. **Sectoral Breakdown**
   - Export sectors supported
   - Non-traditional exports volume

### **Priority 2: Guarantee Programs**
1. **Guarantee Volumes Issued**
   - Total coverage
   - Multiplier effect (private lending enabled)
   - Cost to borrower (interest rate reduction)

2. **Geographic Distribution**
   - Regional concentration
   - Disparity analysis (coastal vs. inland, etc.)

### **Priority 3: Crisis Response**
1. **Time Series 2019-2023**
   - Growth/contraction rates
   - Comparison with private bank behavior
   - COVID-19 response (2020-2021)
