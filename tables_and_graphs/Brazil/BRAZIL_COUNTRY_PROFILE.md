# BRAZIL TRADE FINANCE COUNTRY PROFILE
## January 2012 - December 2024

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Data Coverage:** 838,167 monthly observations
**Period:** January 2012 - December 2024 (156 months)
**Geographic Granularity:** 27 states (UF)
**Sectoral Granularity:** 21 CNAE economic sectors + 100+ subsectors
**Firm Size Granularity:** 4 levels (Micro, Small, Medium, Large)
**Data Source:** Banco Central do Brasil (BCB) - Banking Credit Statistics

**Key Insight:** 99.999% of records correspond to "PJ - Comércio exterior" (Foreign Trade), making this the most comprehensive trade finance dataset in Latin America.

## 1. MARKET STRUCTURE

### 1.1 Trade Finance by Firm Size

Brazil's trade finance market shows extreme concentration in large corporations:

| Firm Size | Records | % Records | Active Portfolio (BRL bn) | % Portfolio |
|-----------|---------|-----------|---------------------------|-------------|
| **Large Companies** | 221,235 | 26.4% | **20,759** | **78.1%** |
| **Medium Companies** | 448,617 | 53.5% | 4,281 | 16.1% |
| **Small Companies** | 130,553 | 15.6% | 585 | 2.2% |
| **Micro Companies** | 33,789 | 4.0% | 638 | 2.4% |
| **Size Unavailable** | 3,972 | 0.5% | 319 | 1.2% |

**Key Findings:**
- Large companies account for **78.1%** of the trade finance portfolio with only **26.4%** of operations
- Average loan size for large companies: **BRL 93.8 million**
- Average loan size for micro companies: **BRL 18.9 million**
- **SME financing gap:** Small and micro companies represent 19.6% of records but only 4.6% of portfolio value

**Maturity Insights:**
- **64.5%** of trade finance has maturity under 1 year (short-term)

- Average maturity: **487 days** (1.3 years)

## 5. CREDIT QUALITY INDICATORS

### 5.1 Non-Performing Loans (NPL) Metrics

| NPL Indicator | Total (BRL bn) | % of Active Portfolio |
|---------------|---------------|----------------------|
| **Overdue >15 days** | 200 | 0.75% |
| **Rolled-over NPL** | 160 | 0.60% |
| **Problem assets** | 540 | 2.03% |

**Credit Quality Assessment:**
- Brazil's trade finance NPL ratio (0.75%) is **significantly lower** than the overall corporate credit NPL (3-4%)
- Problem assets rate (2.03%) indicates moderate credit risk
- Strong credit quality reflects the self-liquidating nature of trade finance and collateral (goods in transit)

### 5.2 NPL Distribution by Firm Size

**Risk Profile:** Default risk increases inversely with firm size, with micro companies showing 3.6x higher NPL rates than large corporations.

## 11. KEY INSIGHTS & RECOMMENDATIONS

### 11.1 Strengths
✅ **Exceptional data granularity** (state, sector, firm size, maturity)
✅ **Strong credit quality** (0.75% NPL rate)
✅ **High trade coverage** (67.4% TF/Trade ratio)
✅ **Sectoral diversification** across 21 economic sectors
✅ **Long time series** (156 months of consistent data)

### 11.2 Challenges
⚠️ **SME access gap:** Small and micro firms represent only 4.6% of portfolio
⚠️ **Geographic concentration:** São Paulo accounts for 48.4% of market
⚠️ **Short-term bias:** 64.5% of portfolio matures within 1 year
⚠️ **Limited foreign currency exposure:** Only 1.0% USD-indexed

### 11.3 Policy Recommendations

1. **SME Support Programs**
   - Develop guarantee schemes to increase small firm access to TF
   - Target: Increase SME share from 4.6% to 10% by 2027

2. **Regional Expansion**
   - Incentivize TF provision in underdeveloped regions (North/Northeast)
   - Reduce São Paulo's market share from 48.4% to 40% by 2028

3. **Maturity Extension**
   - Promote medium-term TF products (1-3 years)
   - Support capital goods imports and export financing

4. **Digitalization**
   - Implement digital trade finance platforms
   - Reduce operational costs for smaller transactions

## 12. DATA QUALITY & METHODOLOGY

### 12.1 Data Source
- **Provider:** Banco Central do Brasil (BCB)
- **System:** SCR (Credit Information System)
- **Frequency:** Monthly
- **Granularity:** Bank × State × Sector × Firm Size × Month

### 12.2 Key Variables
- `data_base`: Reference date (monthly)
- `uf`: Brazilian state (27 federative units)
- `cnae_secao`: CNAE sector (21 main sectors)
- `cnae_subclasse`: CNAE subsector (100+ categories)
- `porte`: Firm size (Micro/Small/Medium/Large)
- `modalidade`: Credit type (PJ - Comércio exterior = 99.999%)
- `carteira_ativa`: Active portfolio (thousands BRL)
- `numero_de_operacoes`: Number of operations
- Maturity buckets: 6 ranges from 0-90 days to >15 years
- NPL indicators: Overdue, rolled-over, problem assets

### 12.3 Data Completeness
- **Coverage:** 99.999% of records are "Foreign Trade" credit
- **Missing values:** <0.1% for main variables
- **Consistency:** High consistency across time series
- **ETL Process:** `Scripts/brasil_etl.R` applies PTAX conversion and BACI trade data merge

## APPENDIX: TECHNICAL DEFINITIONS

### A.1 CNAE Sector Codes
CNAE (Classificação Nacional de Atividades Econômicas) is Brazil's standard industrial classification system, equivalent to ISIC (International Standard Industrial Classification).

### A.2 Firm Size Classification (BCB)
- **Micro:** Annual revenue up to BRL 360K
- **Small:** Annual revenue BRL 360K - BRL 4.8M
- **Medium:** Annual revenue BRL 4.8M - BRL 300M
- **Large:** Annual revenue above BRL 300M

### A.3 NPL Definitions
- **Overdue >15 days:** Credits past due for more than 15 days
- **Rolled-over NPL:** Restructured non-performing loans
- **Problem assets:** Sum of overdue >90 days + doubtful + losses
