# CHILE TRADE FINANCE COUNTRY PROFILE
## January 2015 - December 2024

---

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

**Data Coverage:** 762,687 monthly observations (2015-2024)
**Period:** January 2015 - December 2024 (120 months)
**Bank Coverage:** 26 financial institutions
**Account Coverage:** 882 active accounting codes
**Data Source:** Comisión para el Mercado Financiero (CMF) - Banking Financial Statements

**Key Insight:** Chile has the longest historical trade finance data series in Latin America (26 years available from 1998), with detailed breakdown by type of operation (exports, imports, third-country trade).

## 6. TRADE FINANCE VS. INTERNATIONAL TRADE

### 6.1 TF Coverage Ratio

| Metric | Value (2024) | Source |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| **Total TF portfolio** | USD 1,612 million | CMF (CLP 1,534 bn ÷ 951.7) |
| **Chile exports (2024)** | USD 94,287 million | BACI/CEPII |
| **Chile imports (2024)** | USD 89,654 million | BACI/CEPII |
| **Total trade (X+M)** | USD 183,941 million | BACI/CEPII |
| **TF/Trade ratio** | **0.88%** | Calculation |

**Alarming Finding:**
- Chile's TF/Trade ratio (**0.88%**) is **dramatically lower** than Brazil (67.4%) and Peru (18.7%)
- Indicates **severe under-reporting** in CMF data or alternative financing channels

**Possible Explanations:**
1. **Classification issue:** Much TF classified under "Commercial Credits" (broader category)
2. **Corporate direct financing:** Large mining companies (Codelco, BHP) use corporate credit lines
3. **Foreign bank financing:** Chilean importers/exporters financed by foreign banks (off-shore)
4. **Factoring dominance:** High use of factoring companies (not captured in CMF banking data)
5. **Export credit agencies:** CORFO and other agencies provide direct TF outside banking system

**Recommendation:** Cross-reference with Chilean Central Bank's "Informe de Comercio Exterior" and interview major banks to identify reporting gaps.

## 8. REGULATORY ENVIRONMENT

### 8.1 CMF Reporting Requirements

- **Frequency:** Monthly submission (by 15th of following month)
- **Format:** Standardized chart of accounts (FECU - Ficha Estadística Codificada Uniforme)
- **Granularity:** Account-level (no firm size or geographic breakdown)
- **Transparency:** High - all data publicly available via CMF API
- **Enforcement:** Strong - non-compliance results in sanctions

### 8.2 Capital Requirements (Basel III)

| Asset Type | Risk Weight | Capital Requirement |
|------------|-------------|---------------------|
| **Export credits (insured)** | 20% | 1.6% (20% × 8%) |
| **Import credits (unsecured)** | 100% | 8.0% |
| **Letters of credit** | 20-50% | 1.6-4.0% |
| **Trade guarantees** | 50% | 4.0% |

**Regulatory Advantage:** Chile's adoption of Basel III benefits TF through favorable risk weights for insured export credits.

## 9. KEY INSIGHTS & RECOMMENDATIONS

### 9.1 Strengths
✅ **Longest time series** in LatAm (26 years, 1998-2024)
✅ **Detailed operation breakdown** (export/import/third-country)
✅ **Strong regulatory framework** (CMF transparency)
✅ **Currency granularity** (CLP/USD/indexed)
✅ **Competitive banking sector** (HHI=764)
✅ **Export-oriented** (export financing 27% higher than imports)

### 9.2 Challenges
⚠️ **No firm size data** (cannot analyze SME access)
⚠️ **No geographic breakdown** (cannot identify regional disparities)
⚠️ **No direct sectoral data** (mining vs agriculture unclear)
⚠️ **Low TF/Trade ratio** (0.88% indicates data gaps or off-balance financing)
⚠️ **Limited LC usage** (7.9% of TF, below international standards of 15-20%)

### 9.3 Data Quality Issues

**Critical Finding:** The extremely low TF/Trade ratio (0.88%) suggests:
1. **Under-reporting:** Significant TF classified under generic "Commercial Credits"
2. **Off-shore financing:** Chilean companies financed by foreign banks
3. **Non-bank TF:** Factoring, export credit agencies (CORFO), corporate credit

**Recommendation:** Conduct deep-dive study with:
- Chilean Central Bank trade finance surveys
- Interview top 10 exporters/importers on financing sources
- Analyze factoring industry data (ACHEF - Chilean Factoring Association)
- Map CORFO export credit agency portfolio

### 9.4 Policy Recommendations

1. **Improve Data Granularity**
   - Mandate firm size reporting (at least SME vs Large)
   - Add optional sectoral classification (ISIC codes)
   - Require geographic breakdown (by region)

2. **Promote Letter of Credit Usage**
   - Educate SMEs on LC benefits for international trade
   - Subsidize LC fees for small exporters
   - Target: Increase LC share from 7.9% to 15% by 2027

3. **SME Trade Finance Initiative**
   - Create CORFO guarantee program for SME trade finance
   - Partner with BancoEstado to expand TF to regions
   - Set target: USD 500M TF for SMEs by 2026

4. **Digitalization Push**
   - Implement blockchain-based trade finance platform
   - Integrate customs data with banking systems
   - Reduce documentation requirements (electronic bills of lading)

## APPENDIX: TECHNICAL DEFINITIONS

### A.1 CMF Accounting System
Chile uses FECU (Ficha Estadística Codificada Uniforme), a standardized chart of accounts:
- **1xxxxx:** Assets
- **2xxxxx:** Liabilities
- **8xxxxx:** Contingent assets (off-balance)

### A.2 Currency Types
- **MonedaExtranjera:** Foreign currency (primarily USD)
- **MonedaChilenaNoReajustable:** Chilean Peso, nominal
- **MonedaReajustable:** Inflation-indexed (UF or IPC)
- **MonedaReajustablePorTipoDeCambio:** FX-indexed CLP
